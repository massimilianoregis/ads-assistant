Miky Ads Assistant — Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Who operates this application

Miky Ads Assistant is a private, AI-assisted advertising management application intended for the personal Amazon Ads account of Michela Manesco, an independent user. It is not offered as a public advertising management service.

Information and purposes

Subject to Amazon's authorization and applicable API terms, the application may access advertising account identifiers, marketplace information, campaign settings, keywords, search terms, product identifiers and advertising performance metrics. This information is used to review advertising performance and assist with management of the authorized account. Login with Amazon uses OAuth authorization; the application does not need the user's Amazon password. OAuth credentials and tokens are used to authorize API requests.

AI processing and recipients

The application uses a private MCP server and an Amazon Ads API wrapper to support interaction with ChatGPT (OpenAI). Advertising information necessary for a requested analysis may be transmitted to ChatGPT, subject to Amazon's applicable data-use restrictions and the account's ChatGPT data settings. Amazon and OpenAI process information under their respective terms and privacy notices. This application is intended for internal use, not for selling advertising data or providing data to unrelated third parties. API access and data transfers must remain within the permissions granted by Amazon.

Legal basis and retention

Where applicable, personal data is processed to provide the requested functionality and for legitimate interests in managing the operator's advertising account, subject to applicable law. OAuth authorization may be revoked. Information should be retained only as necessary for the stated purposes, security, and applicable legal obligations. Information processed by external providers is also subject to their own retention policies.

Security and user controls

Access is intended to be restricted to authorized users. API credentials and tokens must be protected and must not be included in AI prompts or publicly disclosed. The account holder can revoke the application's authorization through Amazon's account settings. Where applicable, individuals may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of their personal data, or object to processing and complain to their competent data protection authority.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the application operator through the maintainer's GitHub profile. Please do not post credentials or private advertising information publicly.
